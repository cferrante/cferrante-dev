## Binary extraction

At Thomson Reuters Tax & Accounting, Colleen took over a C/C++ tool that extracted data from
competitors' binary files so customers could switch to Thomson Reuters' tax software. She fixed
its memory leaks and added systematic pattern matching, raising extraction from about half the
fields to nearly all, including high-value fields such as Social Security numbers. She was
offered a full-time role at the end of the contract.

## Web app slowdown

Colleen was brought in to unblock a stalled corporate C# web application. Within the first week
she traced its severe UI lag to memory leaks, and cut page responses from seconds to
milliseconds. Users stopped reporting lag.
