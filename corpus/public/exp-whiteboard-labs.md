## Client services

At Whiteboard Labs, later acquired by SHR, Colleen kept the services revenue flowing so the rest
of the company could build its hospitality app, the product behind the acquisition. She was part
of a team launching 5–15 new client websites a week, including wiring up the educational games
for kids of the State Bar of Texas Law-Related Education Department.

## Concept app

Bridgeway Software needed its concept app, based on David Allen's Getting Things Done, built fast
enough to decide whether to launch it in-house or drop it, and asked for a Whiteboard engineer on
site. Colleen was chosen for her delivery speed, and moved the app quickly enough for Bridgeway
to decide to bring it in-house.
