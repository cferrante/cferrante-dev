## Summary

Colleen Ferrante is a staff software engineer and technical partner with more than 20 years of
experience. She owns the systems a business can't run without and makes them trustworthy: She
restarted a broken CRM in one week without a rewrite, then ran it for ten years as the firm's
only engineer. She removes bottlenecks by passing knowledge on through tools, training and
handovers, so work that depended on one person no longer does. Earlier, in telecom, she built
call-data ingestion and call-center automation. She automates what's certain and sends the rest
to a person, from deduplicating 43,800+ candidate records to building an agentic RAG assistant
with AI coding agents.

## Looking for

Colleen is looking for a role as a staff software engineer: owning a system
the business depends on, end to end, and making it trustworthy for the people who use it.

## Working style

Colleen often says: "Every great engineering solution starts by understanding the user's
friction." Work starts with the problem as the people living it see it, before any design or
code. On cferrante.dev that approach shows in the process itself: a short design spec agreed
before code, a plan whose every step names the test that must fail first, and a written record
of every change of plan and every review finding, with the reason for each.
