## Reporting automation

At Bridgeway Software, eCounsel customers who bought BusinessObjects reporting universes all
waited on the one person who could build them. Colleen taught herself to build universes, then
wrote an internal tool that automated most of the process, so client services reps could build
them and the expert handled only advanced setups. Turnaround fell from weeks to days.

## After the acquisition

Colleen wrote custom Java business rules for enterprise clients of eCounsel, Bridgeway's
matter-management product. After Mitratech acquired Bridgeway, she was the first Bridgeway
engineer working in Mitratech's competing product, and partnered with another engineer to pilot
a training program that taught clients to write their own rules.

## Bringing development in-house

Bridgeway first hired Colleen on contract to bring its concept app's development in-house. She
trained a Bridgeway developer to take it over, and was then offered a full-time role.
