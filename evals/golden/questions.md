# Golden questions: draft for the resume documents

The questions visitors are likely to ask about Colleen's experience, written before the documents
that answer them (plan step 6.1). Each becomes a line in `public.jsonl` once its document exists
and its chunk ids are real; then it is deleted from here. Questions about the documents that
already exist are in `public.jsonl`. Invitee questions live in the private repo's `golden/`.

Wording is a visitor's, not a heading's: a question that copies its heading would pass hit@5
and prove nothing. **Problem** rows are what the welcome invites: a visitor describing their own
bottleneck.

Remapped 2026-11-10 to the resume's current sections: the Whiteboard enterprise account and the
AI monorepo project are gone from the resume, so their questions are dropped; deduplication,
email archiving, the Bridgeway reporting tool and the move to Mitratech's product are new. The
resume's Applied AI section is this site, which `proj-cferrante-dev` already covers.
